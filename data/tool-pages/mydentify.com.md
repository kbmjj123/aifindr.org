The checker describes finding out whether ChatGPT and Claude can reach the pages you want them to use, and spotting access rules that may keep a product out of AI discovery.

## Hands-on run

The page exposed 0 file inputs, 1 text input and 8 buttons, with 1 visible input. We filled the URL field with a synthetic example address and clicked "Check AI crawler access". The page then displayed **"server error"**, which our failure-text detector caught. The requests recorded around the click were Google Analytics beacons and an Ahrefs analytics event — no report response from the checker itself. Both screenshots were saved.

## Verification status
