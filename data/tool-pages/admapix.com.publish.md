# AdMapix

AdMapix is an ad creative intelligence platform aimed at game studios, AI app teams and ecommerce growth teams. According to the vendor, it indexes 200M+ ads across 100+ channels and 170+ market labels, supports search by app, advertiser, keyword, country, channel, format and launch window, and offers a hosted research workflow that produces cited reports, creative-pattern analysis, scripts and UGC briefs. A separate Agent Skill is described as returning structured ad, app, ranking, download, revenue and market data to compatible AI assistants.

The publisher states that new accounts receive 30 lifetime free credits with no payment method required. The public pricing page lists Free, Starter, Pro and Enterprise tiers with 300 / 2,000 / 4,000 monthly credits on the paid plans; figures shown across the vendor's own pages and third-party listings differ, so please check the live pricing page for current prices.

AdMapix publishes a methodology page (v1.1, 2026-08-11) stating that prices are in USD, that one credit does not map uniformly to one search, that media labels describe source placements rather than a count of distinct ad networks, and that there is no publicly verified hourly SLA. Its terms note that ad intelligence data comes from public and third-party providers and may be incomplete or delayed, and that impression, download and revenue figures are estimates rather than official data.

Detailed features, supported platforms, tags, prices and screenshots are shown on the listing page; consult the official site for the current feature set.
