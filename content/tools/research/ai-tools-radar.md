AI Tools Radar helps users explore and compare AI products, model profiles, reusable agent skills, and prompts in ten languages. It also publishes industry news and newsletters.

Public directory and article browsing are free. Vendor submissions and promotion are separate paid services. Listed third-party products have their own pricing.
