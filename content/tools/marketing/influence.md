Influence keeps the working social post and its publishing date in the same planning flow. Creators and small businesses can use a campaign brief and saved Brand Guidelines to draft editable AI captions, choose reusable images or videos and inspect each connected account’s post preview. Confirm the schedule after reviewing the content, then use the social media calendar to move or cancel upcoming posts as the campaign develops. It is a web app for people comparing a social media planner, AI caption writer or post scheduling tool. Plans start at US$19/month or US$190/year; the seven-day trial requires billing details and becomes paid unless canceled. No ongoing free plan.
