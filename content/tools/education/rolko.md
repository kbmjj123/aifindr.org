Rolko provides word lookup on selectable Chinese, Japanese and Korean webpage text through its desktop Chrome extension. Selected words can be saved and reviewed with spaced repetition in the web app. It includes AI-assisted features.

Basic lookup is free. Saving and spaced-repetition review require a paid plan. The homepage includes a public Chinese lookup demonstration; this is not a Japanese or Korean lesson. Rolko is a supplementary reading and vocabulary tool, not a complete language course.

Submitted by the Rolko team. No sponsorship, paid placement or affiliate commission is proposed.
