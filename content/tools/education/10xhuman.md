10xhuman helps aspiring solo founders turn a problem into a structured project. A free three-question brief provides a starting point without signup. The full learning workflow uses AI advisers across 16 stages to develop decisions, a product requirements document, and instructions learners can use with a coding agent.

Learners implement their product and submit its working URL, their own contribution, and verification evidence for review. Document generation alone does not constitute a completed product or a reviewed completion certificate.

The interface supports Korean, English, and Japanese. The standard online course is KRW 299,000 for 12 months. A pilot is recruiting 100 free testers: operator-approved participants receive three months of course access and a free completion certificate after meeting the review criteria. Public testimonials are optional.
