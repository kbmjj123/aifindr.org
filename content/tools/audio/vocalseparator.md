VocalSeparator is a browser-based AI vocal and instrumental separation tool. Users can upload audio they have the right to use, preview the separated tracks, and download WAV files from the same page.

Free use is anonymous, with daily and file-size limits. Local playback controls support looping, tempo, and pitch adjustment for music practice. Audio processing runs in the cloud, and results are temporary.
