Aimusicgen is an AI music generator that turns a text description or your own lyrics into a full song with vocals or an instrumental, right in your browser.

Aimusicgen turns ideas into music: describe a mood, story or sound, or paste your own lyrics, then pick a genre and choose vocals or instrumental. Beyond text-to-music it includes a free lyrics generator, Lyrics to Song, an AI Music Maker (genre/mood/length), and an AI Song Cover tool that re-imagines an uploaded reference track in a new style or with new lyrics. Sign in to get 4 free credits every day (enough for one song), then download your tracks from My Creations; paid plans add more generations, longer storage and commercial-use rights.

Pricing: Freemium; starting $14.90/mo.
