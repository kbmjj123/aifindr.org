SFXMint AI Studio turns text descriptions into sound effects. Compare four takes, trim or loop a selected take, and download WAV files. Audio is AI-generated or procedurally synthesized and offered under CC0.

The sound library is free without signup. Studio offers one guest generation and three free tries with an account, followed by paid passes or plans. Payments do not automatically renew. See https://sfxmint.com/pricing for current limits and prices.

Library search and downloads are also available through a no-key HTTP API and remote MCP server: https://sfxmint.com/api/docs.
