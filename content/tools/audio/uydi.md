Uydi is a browser-based AI voice studio for narration, video voiceovers, podcasts, and character dialogue. Users can choose a library voice, design a personal voice, or clone a voice they have permission to use, then generate speech from text.

Voice Canvas organizes scripts into segments with different speakers and adjustable pauses. Completed segments can be reused, and the full project can be downloaded as one WAV audio file. Uydi offers free credits, a paid subscription, and optional credit top-ups.
