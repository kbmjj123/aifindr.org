Find My Vocal Range is a free online vocal range test for singers and the curious. Hold each note for a second from your lowest to your highest comfortable pitch and get your usable range plus your closest voice type among soprano, mezzo-soprano, contralto, tenor, baritone and bass — with two runners-up and practical tips for warm-ups, song choice and key changes. All pitch detection happens in your browser; no recording, no upload, no account, no cost.
