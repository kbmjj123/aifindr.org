AI Remove Watermark is a browser-based image cleanup tool for JPG, PNG, and WebP files. It supports images up to 10 MB and 4096 pixels, uses one credit per successful image, refunds credits for failed tasks, and keeps results available for 48 hours. Users should upload only images they own or are authorized to edit.
