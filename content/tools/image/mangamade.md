MangaMade turns original stories into manga, manhua and webtoon chapters. It helps
writers and comic creators plan scripts, build character model sheets, generate
panels with consistent characters, and edit dialogue on a separate lettering
layer in more than 30 languages.

Chapters export as PNG pages, PDF, CBZ or vertical webtoon strips. MangaMade is
available on the web and mobile and offers a REST API and MCP server.

New accounts receive 15 free trial credits valid for 30 days. Paid plans start at
$19 per month. Planning, editing, lettering and exporting are free; credits pay
for generated images.
