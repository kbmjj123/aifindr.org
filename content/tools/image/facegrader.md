FaceGrader is a free AI attractiveness test that scores your face 0–100 from a photo. It measures 478 facial landmarks entirely on your device across four fixed dimensions — symmetry (35%), facial thirds (25%), facial fifths (25%) and golden ratio (15%) — then shows the exact formula behind the number. No photo is ever uploaded, no measurements are stored on any server, and no sign-up is required. Frozen engine v1.0: the same photo always returns the same score.
