MeltDots maps photos and artwork to supported fuse-bead palettes, including Perler,
Hama, Artkal and MARD. Makers can adjust grid size and color count, edit individual
beads, replace colors, and check color codes and bead quantities before making a
project.

Direct image conversion and editing are free in the browser without signing in.
Downloading a PNG of a custom pattern requires a free account. Optional AI image
generation and enhancement require an account and use credits.
