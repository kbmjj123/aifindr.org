AUTOMATER.ai is a small tray app for Windows, macOS (Apple silicon) and Linux. It reads the session files AI coding tools already write and puts them into one local, searchable library (41 AI apps and CLIs, 45 session formats, including Claude Code, Codex and Cursor), flags the agents that are waiting on you, and tracks usage across 9 providers in one panel. It also includes free on-device dictation. The free version needs no account; optional PRO is $15/year.
