Live Face Swap is a browser workspace for rehearsing an on-camera character. Choose a built-in look or an image you have permission to use, open the local camera preview, and check framing and lighting before starting AI generation.

Camera preview is free and stays on the device. Live AI generation requires sign-in and account credits; the reference and camera video are sent to the selected model service during processing. Monthly credit plans start at $9.90. Review the displayed engine rate before starting, and end the AI session when finished: generation consumes credits even while you are not recording.

Supported browsers can record, replay and save clips locally. Microphone audio can be included with permission. The [AI webcam filter workspace](https://live-face-swap.net/live-restyle) explores visual treatments across the scene. For clothing references, [live outfit inspiration](https://live-face-swap.net/live-try-on) offers a camera-based styling view without predicting physical fit or garment size.

Free local preview does not include free live AI generation. Browser recording and downloads do not add an AI processing charge.
