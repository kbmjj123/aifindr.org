AI Video Extender makes a 6-second 480p AI video and extends an original AI video to 12 seconds. Describe a shot and get a 6s 480p clip; when a clip ends too soon, direct the next 6 seconds in one click, which produces new frames rather than a loop. One action per generation or extension, and the first action is free once an email is verified.

Typical uses are product demos, social posts and travel clips that need a little more running time.

Pricing: Freemium; one free action after email verification, then one-time packs of 20 video actions for $19 or 50 actions for $39. No subscription and no recurring charge; purchased actions do not expire.
