FORMWEFT Motion Workbench lets users import a local video and an existing SRT subtitle file, edit animated captions and cards, adjust timing, and export a video with its original audio. Basic local editing is free and does not require sign-in; imported videos are not automatically uploaded.

MP4 export depends on browser support, with WebM as a fallback. Export runs in real time and requires keeping the page open. Optional AI generation requires a configured cloud provider or local setup and may incur separate provider costs; it is not included in the free local editing claim. The tool does not automatically transcribe imported video.

Usage guide: https://formweft.com/resources/formweft-motion-workbench
