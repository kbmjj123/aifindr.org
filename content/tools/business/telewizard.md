TeleWizard provides managed AI phone agents that answer calls, collect intake details, book appointments, and route requests to staff. Law firms can configure the service around their intake questions, booking rules, and escalation workflows. The TeleWizard team sets up and maintains the service and can connect workflows with Clio, Lawmatics, and scheduling tools.

AI Supervisor reviews conversations for communication gaps and business-process findings. Warm transfers bring context to staff, and the agent can ask a team member for information before continuing a conversation. TeleWizard supports more than 50 languages and offers web chat and messaging integrations. Pricing is paid and based on a tailored quotation; there is no standard free plan or trial.
