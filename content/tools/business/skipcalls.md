SkipCalls answers business phone calls, takes messages, books appointments, and transfers callers to the right person. It helps small businesses handle incoming calls when the owner is busy or unavailable.

Businesses can keep their existing number through call forwarding and manage calls through the web dashboard or mobile app. The receptionist uses business information to answer routine questions and sends call summaries so the team can follow up.

Paid subscriptions are available with a seven-day free trial. Current plans and details are available on the SkipCalls website.
