iPulse AI is an Open Agentic Investment Research Platform built by Future Edge Group. It combines market intelligence, ranked Top Picks, asset forecasts and independent AI advisor reports so investors and investment teams can compare perspectives and inspect evidence, assumptions and limitations.

Open means inspectable research and methods; it does not imply that all code, proprietary data or production infrastructure is open source. The platform supports research and decision support rather than guaranteed returns or autonomous financial advice.
